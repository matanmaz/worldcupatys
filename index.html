What is this?

The 2026 men's World Cup is the biggest ever — 48 teams, hosted across the US, Canada, and Mexico. World Cup @ YS is a grassroots effort to bring Yellow Springs together to watch it, on a big outdoor screen, near the Bryan Center, all summer long.

We're in the planning phase right now. Three ways you can help shape it:

A 96-Year Global Tradition

The FIFA World Cup has been played since 1930 — through wars, recessions, and revolutions. From Uruguay's first triumph in Montevideo to Maradona's "Hand of God" in Mexico City, from Zinedine Zidane's headbutt in Berlin to Messi's coronation in Lusail — the tournament is, for one month every four years, the closest thing humanity has to a shared experience.

In 2026 it comes to North America for the third time. And for the first time, it sprawls across three host countries and 48 nations. Yellow Springs is going to feel it too.

The Party

Two different vibes for two different stages of the tournament — both centered downtown, near the Bryan Center.

Where

We're aiming for the green space by the Bryan Community Center in downtown Yellow Springs — central, walkable, family-friendly, and big enough to handle a real crowd. Final location pending permits and a few key phone calls (👀 see the Volunteer page).

Two Phases, One Summer

Group Stage · June 11–27

Casual drop-in viewing. Big screen up, blankets and lawn chairs welcome, a few games per day. Bring your own snacks, a kid, a dog, a neighbor. Low key. Come and go.

We'll screen the matches the community votes for — head to the Vote page to pick yours.

Knockouts · June 28 → July 19

The full party. Quarterfinals, semis, and the final get the works:

  • 🍲 Potluck — bring a dish from somewhere on the map
  • 🎧 DJ + dance party before and after the match
  • 🎨 Kids zone — face paint, mini-pitch, crafts
  • 📺 Big screen with proper sound
