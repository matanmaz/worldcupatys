A Tournament Almost a Century Old

The FIFA World Cup has been bringing the planet together since Uruguay 1930 — 13 teams in Montevideo, hand-stitched leather balls, and a final watched by 68,000 people. Ninety-six years later, the tournament arrives on our doorstep: 48 nations, three host countries (USA, Mexico, Canada), and matches in Cincinnati just up the road. From Pelé's barefoot childhood in Bauru to Maradona's "Hand of God," from the Miracle of Bern to Mbappé's hat-trick — every World Cup writes a new chapter in the only story the whole world reads at once. We want Yellow Springs to be part of that story.

Why Yellow Springs?

Because this village already knows how to throw a street party. Because we have a lawn by the Bryan Center, a community that shows up, and a soft spot for any event involving potluck. Because soccer is — finally and undeniably — an American sport too, and our kids deserve to grow up remembering where they watched the goals.

This is grassroots. Not for profit. Built by neighbors.

The Watch Party

Two tiers of gathering. Mellow daytime watches for the group stage. Full-village blowouts for the knockouts.

📍 The Location

We're aiming to set up by the Bryan Community Center in downtown Yellow Springs — the lawn gives us shade, the building gives us power and bathrooms, and downtown gives us walk-up neighbors. (Pending village approval — see the Volunteer page if you can help with that conversation.)

🥣 Group Stage — Daytime Hangs

From June 11 through June 27, the group stage runs 72 matches over 17 days. We'll pick a curated handful — guided by your votes — and show them as low-key community watches. Bring a chair, bring snacks, stick around or drop in. Coffee in the morning, cold drinks in the afternoon.

🎉 Knockouts — The Big Parties

Starting with the Round of 32 (June 28) and especially from the Quarterfinals (July 9–11) onward, we go big:

🍲Potluck

Bring a dish from your favorite country still in it.

🧒Kids Zone

Face painting, mini-pitch, flag crafts, sidewalk chalk.

🎧DJ Set

Pre-match warm-up and full-throttle post-match dance party.

💃Dance Floor

Win or lose, we celebrate. Cumbia, samba, afrobeats, everything.

🗓️ Dates to Hold

  • Thu Jun 11 — Opening match (Mexico City)
  • Jun 11 – Jun 27 — Group stage (72 games, we screen a chosen few)
  • Jun 28 – Jul 3 — Round of 32
  • Jul 4 – Jul 7 — Round of 16
  • Jul 9 – Jul 11 — Quarterfinals → first big party
  • Jul 14 & 15 — Semifinals
  • Sat Jul 18 — Third place match
  • Sun Jul 19 — FINAL at MetLife Stadium, NJ
Heads up: Final date, location, and screening list all depend on village permits, weather, and volunteer power. Sign up to help and we'll keep you in the loop.
