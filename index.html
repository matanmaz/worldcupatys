Why a Village-Wide Watch Party?

Since 1930, the World Cup has been the planet's biggest shared moment — a tournament that connects neighborhoods from Buenos Aires to Dakar to Tokyo. In 2026 the games come to North America for the first time in 32 years, with matches just a few hours' drive away. We think Yellow Springs is exactly the kind of place to gather around the world's game.

A Tournament With a Long Memory

From Uruguay's improbable 1930 triumph in Montevideo, to Pelé's Brazil in '70, to Maradona's '86, to the U.S.-hosted summer of '94, to Spain's tiki-taka in 2010, to Argentina lifting the cup in Qatar in 2022 — the World Cup is a living archive of how the world has changed, and how it still gathers. In 2026, North America hosts for the first time across three countries: Mexico, Canada, and the United States. We get to be a small, joyful node in that global moment.

The Watch Party Plan

We're aiming for two distinct experiences: low-key community screenings during the group stage, and full-blown block parties from the quarterfinals on.

📍 Where

The Bryan Center Lawn — Downtown Yellow Springs

Our target location is the area around the Bryan Community Center in downtown YS. It's central, walkable, has parking nearby, room for a screen and a dance floor, and is already the heart of village events. Permission is still being worked out — see the Volunteer page if you can help make calls.

⚽ Group Stage Screenings (June 11–27)

Casual. BYO chair or blanket. We screen the games the community voted for — projector on an outdoor screen, sound piped through a small PA, snacks and drinks from whoever feels like bringing them. Family-friendly during day matches.
